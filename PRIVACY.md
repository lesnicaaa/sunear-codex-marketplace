# Privacy Notice

The Sunear Designer plugin is publicly installable. Use of the Sunear service is invite-only and requires OAuth consent from an invited account.

Codex reads selected source documents in the user's local environment. The plugin sends Sunear bounded facts and source evidence required for the requested project operation, such as explicitly extracted dimensions, product intent, quantities, and source references. Formal quotation bytes are returned from Sunear to Codex only when the user requests an export.

Submitted facts are processed by the Sunear service to validate, create, read, or revise the invited organization's project. The organization controls which documents Codex reads and should avoid selecting unrelated or unnecessary personal information.

OAuth tokens and capability-bearing Review Links are secrets. They must not be included in prompts, documents, URLs other than the issued Review Link itself, logs, analytics, public issues, or support messages. Use the Sunear account connection controls or contact the Sunear administrator for access revocation, retention, or privacy requests.
