# Privacy Notice

The Sunear Designer plugin is publicly installable. Use of the Sunear service is invite-only and requires an administrator-issued organization key.

Codex reads selected source documents in the user's local environment. The plugin is designed to send Sunear only bounded facts required for the requested project operation, such as explicitly extracted dimensions, product intent, quantities, and source references. Original document bytes are not sent to Sunear by this plugin.

Submitted facts are processed by the Sunear service to validate, create, read, or revise the invited organization's project. The organization controls which documents Codex reads and should avoid selecting unrelated or unnecessary personal information.

Authentication keys and capability-bearing Review Links are secrets. They must not be included in prompts, documents, URLs other than the issued Review Link itself, logs, analytics, public issues, or support messages. Contact the issuing Sunear administrator for access revocation, rotation, retention, or privacy requests.
